Featured Stories

House of the Dragon

House of the Dragon depicts an intense succession struggle within House Targaryen, set nearly 200 years before Game of Thrones, as rival factions vie for the Iron Throne with armies and dragons.

Game Of Thrones

Game of Thrones unfolds across the continents of Westeros and Essos, where rival noble families vie for the Iron Throne amid political intrigue, shifting alliances, and an ancient supernatural threat beyond the Wall.

The First Law

The First Law follows a group of warriors, nobles, and outcasts caught in a growing conflict, as kingdoms compete for power through war, alliances, betrayal, and manipulation.

The Dragon's Path(The dagger and the coin)

The Dagger and the Coin follows rival kingdoms and powerful individuals as war, political schemes, economic power, and shifting alliances threaten to reshape the continent.

The Pillars of the Earth

The Pillars of the Earth follows nobles, clergy, and ordinary people in medieval England as civil war, political rivalries, and struggles for power shape the fate of a kingdom.

The Once and Future King

The Once and Future King follows King Arthur's rise to power as he attempts to unite a divided kingdom while facing political conflicts, rival rulers, betrayal, and war.
